01

Save-card failures

E-6 · E-7 — the moment must never feel like a rejection of the person

Three failure shapes: a field the creator can fix, a decline the bank made, and a 3DS challenge the bank demands. Rule: errors name the card, never the creator ("this card was declined," not "you were declined"), and every error keeps the spot safe — the reservation never dies on a payment hiccup.

02

Stall, waiver & the release moments

E-9 · E-10 · E-16 — the card step as a portal state, not a wall

The card step never blocks what's already done — product picked, spot held. It appears in the portal as one finishable row, and every way it resolves (saved, waived, released, exited) gets its own calm confirmation. The waiver is Katie's move; the portal simply reflects it without drama.

04

The final warning — Katie's moment, on every channel

E-18 · E-20 · E-21 — the spec from rev 4, now with faces

Three renderings of one message: the text (Katie's number, TCPA-clean per E-20), the email (the legally load-bearing copy with the facts table), and the portal banner (where both buttons live). All three repeat the same escape sentence — "one reply is enough" — because E-18 says the likeliest ghost is someone who already posted and never told us.
